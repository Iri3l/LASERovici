# LASERovici – Roadmap

This document outlines long-term goals and feature ideas for future versions of LASERovici.  
Items here are **not yet planned**, but may move into NOTES.md when prioritized.

---

## 🎯 Mid-Term Goals (3–6 months)
- **Dark/Light theme toggle** for better user experience.  
- **Enhanced product descriptions** with storytelling for SEO.  
- **Gift packaging option** at checkout.  
- **Customer subscription form** → connect directly with Mailchimp/Brevo for automated campaigns.  
- **Apple Pay & Google Pay** → expand payment flexibility beyond PayPal.  

---

## 🚀 Long-Term Goals (6–12 months)
- **Loyalty & rewards system** (points for repeat buyers).  
- **Gift card codes** that can be redeemed in the shop.  
- **3D product preview** (laser engraving preview mockup).  
- **Multi-language support** (English + Romanian as start).  
- **Wholesale/B2B section** for bulk orders.  

---

## 💡 Ideas for Future Exploration
- Seasonal **themed shop skins** (Christmas, Valentine’s, etc.).  
- **Referral program** (invite friends, get discounts).  
- Integration with **TikTok Shop / Instagram Shop**.  
- **Blog / Inspiration section** for SEO and brand authority.  
- **Augmented Reality (AR) preview** on mobile (stretch goal).  

---

## 📝 Notes
- Priorities can shift depending on user feedback & sales data.  
- Once an item is ready to be implemented, move it into `NOTES.md` as a *Planned Step*.  
- Completed features will be tracked in `CHANGELOG.md`.
